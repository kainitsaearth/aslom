Our farmers

Grown where the clouds rest.

Lanao is the highest peak in the Philippines. Its volcanic soil, cool mist, and unhurried sun make it one of the rarest places on earth to grow Arabica.

We work directly with twelve smallholder families who have farmed these slopes for three generations. No middlemen. Fair trade prices, paid in advance.
